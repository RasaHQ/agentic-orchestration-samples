Insurance and Payment

Accepted insurance:
The clinic accepts most major insurance plans including BlueCross, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, and Medicare. Contact the billing office at 555-0199 to verify your specific plan before your visit.

Copays and deductibles:
Copays are collected at check-in. If you have not met your annual deductible, you may receive a bill for the remaining balance after insurance processing.

Self-pay patients:
Self-pay visits are accepted. A standard primary care visit is billed at the clinic's self-pay rate. Ask the front desk for current pricing.

Payment methods:
The clinic accepts credit cards, debit cards, HSA/FSA cards, and cash. Payment plans may be available for balances over $500 — ask billing for details.

Referrals and prior authorization:
Some specialist visits and procedures require a referral or prior authorization from your insurance. Bring your referral letter if one was issued.

Billing questions:
For questions about a bill or insurance claim, email billing@example-clinic.com or call 555-0199, Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
