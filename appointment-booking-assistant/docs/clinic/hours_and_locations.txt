Clinic Hours and Locations

Main clinic hours:
The main clinic is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. The clinic is closed on weekends and public holidays.

Main clinic address:
123 Health Street, Medical District, City 10001.

Parking:
Free patient parking is available in the garage adjacent to the main clinic entrance. Validation is provided at check-in.

Public transit:
The clinic is a 5-minute walk from Central Station (Metro Line A). Bus routes 12 and 45 stop directly outside the building.

Urgent same-day concerns:
For urgent medical issues that cannot wait for a scheduled appointment, visit the Urgent Care wing at the same address, open daily from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM, or call the nurse advice line at 555-0100.

After-hours emergencies:
For life-threatening emergencies, call emergency services (911). Do not use this booking assistant for emergency care.
