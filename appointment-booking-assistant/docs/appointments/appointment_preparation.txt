Preparing for Your Appointment

Arrival time:
Please arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time to complete check-in and any required paperwork.

What to bring:
Bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications, and any referral forms if your visit requires one.

First-time patients:
New patients should arrive 20 minutes early to complete registration forms. Bring a summary of your medical history if available.

Follow-up visits:
For follow-up appointments, bring notes on any changes in symptoms since your last visit and a list of questions for your doctor.

Telehealth vs in-person:
This booking assistant schedules in-person clinic visits. If you need a telehealth appointment, ask the front desk about virtual visit availability.

After your visit:
Lab results and follow-up instructions are typically available through the patient portal within 3 to 5 business days after your appointment.
